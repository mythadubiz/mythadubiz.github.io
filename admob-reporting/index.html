Mythadubiz AdMob Reporting

Internal developer tool — not a public app, no end-user download.

What this is

This is a private, internal reporting tool used solely by the developer (Mythadubiz / Thanh My DUONG) to read ad-revenue reports from their own Google AdMob account via the official AdMob API. It is not distributed, not published on any app store, and not used by any third party or end user.

Privacy Policy

This tool accesses only the developer's own AdMob account data (ad revenue, impressions, click statistics for the developer's own apps) through Google's AdMob API, using an OAuth2 authorization the developer grants to themselves. No personal data from any third party, end user, or app player is collected, stored, or processed by this tool. Data retrieved is stored locally on the developer's own machine for personal bookkeeping only, and is never shared with, sold to, or accessed by any other party.

Terms of Use

This tool is used exclusively by its developer for their own AdMob account reporting. It is not offered to, and must not be used by, any other party.
